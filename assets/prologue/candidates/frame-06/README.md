# Frame 06 — seven-layer matter-language candidates

Generated on 2026-07-22 with Codex built-in image generation (`gpt-image-2`).
The locked Observer Core PNG was supplied as a **style-only** reference; the
TC-07 object itself was explicitly excluded from this frame.

## Shared constraints

All candidates attempt to show exactly seven connected scales without text:
quark, nucleon, atom, molecule, cell, life and civilization. Red is limited to
the quark, cyan describes observation and material transfer, green begins at
cell/life, and amber appears only at civilization. The left 42–45% and lower
left remain dark for localized web copy.

## Candidate deltas and assessment

- **A — wide emergence arc:** the most expansive sense of scale and a strong
  material river, but the quark and early stages enter the left title zone.
  Several early forms also read too much like classroom particle icons.
- **B — compact S cascade:** best literal layer count and cleanest empty left
  half, but separate circular containers make it feel like an infographic or
  a string of icons.
- **C — continuous match dissolve:** keeps all seven stages, shares material
  between them, protects the title zone, and reads well on mobile. Strong
  instructional option, but the lower-right life/civilization region is dense.
- **D — expanding topological fold:** carries the same seven-stage order with
  about 35% fewer secondary objects and the strongest single silhouette. It
  keeps desktop copy clear and preserves the central chain in the 68%-anchored
  mobile crop. **Locked by product review on 2026-07-23 as the structure
  master.**
